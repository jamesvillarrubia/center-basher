## §20 — Schumer quote cluster

**Quote wording + date.** "For every blue-collar Democrat we lose in western Pennsylvania,
we will pick up two moderate Republicans in the suburbs in Philadelphia, and you can repeat
that in Ohio and Illinois and Wisconsin." July 2016.
→ **SOLID** on wording and date (widely and consistently quoted). **CAVEAT:** the exact
original venue is not firmly pinned down across sources — reliably dated to late July 2016,
recounted in *The New Republic* (2017) and many outlets, but no single primary transcript
surfaced. Footnote [54] already hedges this ("remarks at a Washington, D.C. event, late
July 2016, widely reported"). Acceptable; could be tightened if a primary source is found.

**"Clinton then lost all three of those states."**
→ 🔴 **WRONG (factual error).** Schumer's sentence names **four** states: Pennsylvania,
Ohio, Illinois, Wisconsin. In 2016 Clinton **lost** Pennsylvania (Trump +0.7), Ohio
(Trump +8.1), and Wisconsin (Trump +0.8) — but **won Illinois by ~17 points**. So "lost all
three" is false, and "those three" is ambiguous (the nearest antecedent is "Ohio and
Illinois and Wisconsin," which includes the one she won).
- Sources: FEC 2016 official results; Ballotpedia; Wikipedia (2016 IL/PA/OH/WI).
- **Body↔footnote inconsistency:** footnote [54] correctly says she lost "Pennsylvania, Ohio,
  and Wisconsin" — the body contradicts its own footnote.
- **Recommended fix:** body → "Clinton then lost Pennsylvania, Ohio, and Wisconsin, carrying
  only Illinois." (matches the footnote; accurate). Logged to BLOCKERS.

## §21 — 2008 superdelegates

**"roughly three-to-one lead among superdelegates" (169–63).**
→ **SOLID, minor caveat.** 169:63 = 2.68:1. "Roughly three-to-one" rounds up; "nearly
three-to-one" or "more than two-to-one" would be tighter. Not worth a blocker on its own.
Sources: AP Dec 2007; NPR (2015) recounting.

**"a year before a vote was cast."**
→ 🔴 **WRONG (timing).** The cited 169–63 count is the AP's **December 2007** count; the
first contest (Iowa caucus) was **January 3, 2008** — about one month later, not a year.
The footnote dates it correctly ("December 2007 count") but the body says "a year before,"
so body↔footnote disagree again.
- **Recommended fix:** body → "before a single vote was cast" (drop "a year"), or
  "weeks before the first contest." Logged to BLOCKERS.

**"Obama overtook her … and the party's insiders fell in behind the voters."**
→ **SOLID.** Obama surpassed Clinton in superdelegates by May 2008 as he led pledged
delegates; superdelegates followed the pledged-delegate winner (AP; Wikipedia 2008 primary).
