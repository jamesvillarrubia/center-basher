**Thesis (framing C).** The **electability argument** — *move to the center to win* — **is a lie.** Three
findings carry it: the center isn't a coherent bloc (~1 in 6 true moderates); positions barely move
voters; the candidate cast as the establishment loses. Trust is **context** (the era + a near-universal
floor), not the engine; the cleavage that *grew* is racialized status realignment, held loosely.

**Structure.** Winning is **two jobs** — *Choice* (picking you) and *Turnout* (showing up), different
drivers, and "move to the center" fails both. Swing/reachable voters → **Choice**; no-shows → **Turnout**.
