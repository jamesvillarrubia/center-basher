## The thesis (honed & data-honest)
We live in a **historically low-trust era** (Pew: trust in government 77% in 1964 → 19% in 2015; sub-30% since 2007). In that era:
- The **whole electorate** sits low on institutional trust; establishment candidates orbit **high**, mismatched with the mass.
- **Left–right still sorts who votes for whom** — but that's *discovery* of a candidate's pre-baked position. **Shifting** ideology ("move to the center") converts ~nobody (Kalla-Broockman persuasion ≈0). Centrism is a **donor / elite-signaling** move, not a vote-winning one.
- **Turnout** isn't ideological; it runs on **engagement / contact / affect** and on "are they on my side."
- **Authenticity is relational** — conferred by institutional *proximity*, not a trait you can manufacture. The same candidate reads honest or fake depending where the voter sits.
- The election is decided mostly by **who is nominated** — their pre-baked institutional position + reputation/visibility. The campaign is **discovery**, not relocation. *Exception:* an unknown candidate **sets** their position while becoming known, then it sticks.
- **Gravity:** pull on the contestable ≈ **visibility (mass) × trust-alignment.** Policy barely enters.

> NOT the thesis (refuted): "trust replaces left-right for vote choice." Cross-sectionally, ideology still sorts choice (except pure independents). The trust story is **era-level + turnout + authenticity + marginal defectors**, not a cross-sectional swap.

## WHERE DATA EXISTS AND IS ALIGNED
1. **Trust collapse** — Pew 77%→19%, sub-30% since 2007. ANES index mean 0.22 ≈ 19% (V161215 = the Pew item).
2. **Turnout ⊥ ideology** (validated turnout ≈0); turnout = engagement/contact ("care who wins" std 0.46; interest 79/54%).
3. **Persuasion ≈0 in generals** — Kalla-Broockman, 49 field experiments.
4. **GOTV causal sizes** — Gerber-Green (canvass +4.3, social-pressure +8.1, policy message ≈0).
5. **Nominee >> campaign** — the 8B scaling (priors ~22pt vs campaign ≈0).
6. **Sanders→Trump defection** — CES vote-validated; exceeded the margin in WI(2.7×)/MI(5.3×)/PA(3.0×); reproduces Schaffner's 12%.
7. **Authenticity crossover** — ANES traits: low-trust see Trump honest/caring, high-trust see Clinton; corr −0.37, survives ideology control.
8. **"Cares about me" = strongest proximate vote predictor**, absorbs trust (caveat: endogenous).
9. **Plank-stealing asymmetry** — Barber-Pope (party follows Trump's cue over ideology); ANES honesty gap (Trump 3.72 rated MORE honest than Clinton 3.95); Lenz follow-the-leader.
10. **Party-of-president trust** — Pew: GOP 47% (Bush) → 13% (Obama); explains the 2016 "low-trust = GOP" confound.
11. **Swing = disaffected not centrist** — Pew Sideliners, Knight nonvoters, ANES moveable bloc converge.
12. **Pure independents** — trust > ideology for their choice.

## WHERE DATA IS WEAK / REFUTED / NEEDS CARE
- "Trust replaces ideology for choice" — REFUTED cross-sectionally (ideology sorts choice except pure indeps). → reframe to era + discovery.
- "Contestable are *distinctively* low-trust" — they're at the (low) average; low-trust is universal. → reframe.
- **Fig 7** trust-defection partly ideology-confounded (didn't control ideology within non-Republicans). → REFRAME honestly.
- Authenticity/cares findings **endogenous** (motivated reasoning) — consistent with "relational/conferred," not a causal lever.
