The claim “Swing voters are the moderate middle. Win them by moving there.”
92% of the voters who actually switched sides are not centrists.
These are the people who crossed party lines in 2016, the exact voters the strategy is aimed at. Nine in ten of them hold at least one hard-left or hard-right position. There is no middle to meet them in.
