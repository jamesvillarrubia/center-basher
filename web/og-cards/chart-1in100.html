The claim “Persuadable / swing voters are moderate or centrist.”
Look centrist: 46. Actually centrist: 1.
Out of 100 voters, judged by the positions they actually hold, not the label they pick. 46 have issue positions that average to the middle; only 8 hold no strong position on anything; and just 1 is centrist on every issue. Self-placement hides this, because people avoid calling themselves extreme even when their positions are.
Not centrist on any measure (54)
Issues average to the middle (46)
… of those, no extreme position (8)
… of those, centrist throughout (1)
Source: ANES 2016 Time Series, POST weight (V160102); 2016 voters answering ≥5 of 6 seven-point issue scales. Averages to the middle = issue mean within 0.75 of center (46.2%). No extreme position = every answered scale in {3,4,5} (7.6%). Centrist on every issue = every answered scale at the exact midpoint (0.72%, the “True Middler”).
