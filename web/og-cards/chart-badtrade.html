The claim “Win swing voters by moving to the center.”
The voters who flipped 2016 were the most anti-establishment.
Distrust of government, by group. The voters who cost Clinton Wisconsin, Michigan and Pennsylvania distrusted the system more than anyone else on the left, and more than the moderates the strategy was chasing. Longer bar, angrier at the system.
Source: ANES 2016, weighted. Distrust = 1 minus the 3-item institutional-trust index (0–1). Group means by 7-pt ideology band. Lean-left swing = lib-con lean-left voters who defected from Clinton in WI/MI/PA.
