The pitch “There’s a big moderate middle — govern to the center.”
The “moderate middle” is an averaging trick.
On any single issue, only 22% of voters pick the center — 78% pick a side. Average opposing sides together and a “moderate” appears who mostly doesn’t exist.
Source: ANES 2016, all individual responses across six 7-pt issue scales, weighted (V160101); n = 3,418 voters.
