The claim “Independents and swing voters sit in the center.”
They don’t. Independents aren’t centrist — they just average to the center.
Each issue position, minus where their average puts them: it undercounts the wings and overcounts the center. 48% hold both a strong-left and strong-right view.
Undercounted — more hold this than the average shows
Overcounted — fewer than the average implies
Source: ANES 2016 — independents + leaners (36% of voters); six 7-pt issue positions vs. their mean; weighted.
