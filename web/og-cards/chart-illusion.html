The “moderate middle” is an averaging trick.
Same voters, same six issues. Average each person’s positions and a tidy center appears (blue). Plot their actual issue-by-issue positions (red) and the mass is at the poles — 26% sit at the extremes, vs. just 0.4% “on average.”
