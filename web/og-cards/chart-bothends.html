The claim “Independents are the moderate voters in the middle.”
9 in 10 independents hold at least one extreme position.
Nearly half hold a hard-left position and a hard-right one at the same time. Average those together and you manufacture a moderate who doesn’t exist.
Source: ANES 2016, weighted (V160101). Independents + leaners (7-pt PID 3–5; 36% of respondents, n=1,232). “Extreme” = a 7-pt issue scale answered at 1–2 or 6–7, across six issues.
