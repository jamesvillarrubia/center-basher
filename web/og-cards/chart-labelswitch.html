The claim “Socialists can’t win, so the party has to run to the center.”
Call it “socialist,” and you never have to argue with the 60% who agree.
The trick has three steps: point at a fringe candidate who loses, staple the word “socialist” onto every policy to its left, then treat the policies as losers too. But the candidate is the strawman. The policies are the target. The label is the switch.
Support: Medicare-for-all 56% and public option 74% (KFF Health Tracking Poll, Jan 2019, n=1,190); raise taxes on incomes over $400k 58% and on large corporations 63% (Pew Research Center, Jan–Feb 2025, n=5,086). Candidate win rates: illustrative, not from a single dataset; self-described socialists have rarely won U.S. House seats in Republican-leaning districts, which is the one true part of the argument.
