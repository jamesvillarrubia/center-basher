The claim “Socialists can’t win, so the party has to run to the center.”
Call it “socialist,” and you never have to argue with the 60% who agree.
Point at a fringe candidate who loses. Staple “socialist” onto every policy to their left. Now the policies look like losers too. But the candidate is the strawman; the policies are the target; the word is the switch.
Support: Medicare-for-all 56% and public option 74% (KFF Health Tracking Poll, Jan 2019, n=1,190); raise taxes on incomes over $400k 58% and on large corporations 63% (Pew Research Center, Jan–Feb 2025, n=5,086). The candidate win rate is illustrative, not from a single dataset: self-described socialists have rarely won U.S. House seats in Republican-leaning districts, which is the one true part of the argument.
