The claim “The voters in play are moderates. Move to the middle and you get them.”
The voters actually in play aren’t centrists. They’re unattached.
Share of each group that calls itself “middle” or won’t place itself on the left-right scale at all. The movable voters are the ones with the weakest grip on that axis, in all three cycles, which is why moving along it doesn’t reach them. Their issue positions aren’t centrist either: 92% hold at least one hard-left or hard-right position.

Source: ANES 2016/2020/2024, POST weights (V160102 / V200010b / V240107b). “No firm position” = self-places at the exact middle of the 7-pt lib-con scale, or answers “haven’t thought much about this.” Movable = crossed party lines, or changed whether they voted since the prior cycle. Independently confirmed in the VSG panel, where switching is observed rather than recalled (2012–2016 switchers 46% middle vs 31% of the electorate).
