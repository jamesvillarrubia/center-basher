The claim “The centrists were right. The left had to come to them.”
The “electable” position keeps moving left. It just arrives late.
The Democratic establishment’s own health platform, cycle by cycle. It has moved steadily toward the position it spent a decade calling unelectable, and the man holding that position never moved at all. Obamacare was the compromise: built on private insurers, with the public option stripped out before passage.
Positions are the documented platforms, not a measured scale, so read the ordering rather than the spacing: ACA as enacted in 2010 (public option dropped in the Senate before passage); Clinton 2016 (defend and build on the ACA, opposed single-payer); Biden 2020 (Medicare buy-in public option, explicitly opposed Medicare-for-all).
