The claim “Those health plans were too far left. That’s why those candidates couldn’t win.”
Every popular health plan runs the same direction. Including the “radical” one.
Four coverage proposals, asked in the same poll with the same wording. Each column is 100 Americans and the filled squares favor it. All four expand government coverage, all four win a majority, and the most sweeping one clears 50% too. Nothing pointing the other way polls anywhere near this.
Source: KFF Health Tracking Poll, fielded January 9–14, 2019, n=1,190 adults; all four items asked in the same survey. KFF’s own caveat applies and matters: support moves sharply when trade-offs are named, and the public splits close to evenly on Medicare-for-all once tax increases or treatment delays are mentioned. Read this as where opinion starts, not where it ends.
