The claim “Voters can tell who’s honest. Clinton just had a likability problem.”
Clinton’s problem wasn’t left-vs-right. It was authenticity.
Low-trust voters decide elections, and most of the country is low-trust. Whether a candidate reads as “honest” flips with the voter’s own trust in government, and Clinton only won that read in the high-trust corner, where almost no one lives.
Source: ANES 2016, PRE weight (V160101). “Is honest” trait rating, 0–4 (higher = more honest), for Clinton (V161162) and Trump (V161167), by trust band on the 3-item government-trust composite (V161215/16/17): low ≤0.08, middle ≤0.25, high >0.25. Band widths are each band’s share of Americans.
