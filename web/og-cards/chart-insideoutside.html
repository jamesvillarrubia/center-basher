The claim “Move to the middle. That’s where the winnable voters are.”
The middle is 6% of voters. Distrust is 75%.
Voters grouped by the positions they actually hold, not the label they pick. Only 6% hold no extreme position at all. The largest single group holds a hard-left and a hard-right position at the same time, which is the “middle” that averaging invents. Every group is majority-distrustful.
Source: ANES 2016 Time Series, POST weight (V160102), n=2,650 voters. Categories from six 7-pt issue scales (≥5 of 6 answered): true centrist = every answered position in {3,4,5}; discordant = holds at least one position at 1–2 and one at 6–7; otherwise left or right by issue mean. Trust = 3-item composite (V161215/V161216/V161217); low = ≤0.25. Block area is proportional to share of voters.
