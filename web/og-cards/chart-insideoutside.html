The claim “The winning move is to position yourself in the middle.”
Nobody cares that Sanders is left of Buttigieg. They care that he’s outside.
Sanders and Trump agree on nothing and share the only trait a low-trust electorate rewards. Buttigieg and Romney agree on nothing either, and share the one it punishes. The horizontal axis is the argument every campaign has. The vertical one decides it.
