Every block is sized to its real share of 2016 voters. Distrust is the dominant fact in all four columns, and the voters who actually switched sides are spread across every one of them.
Source: ANES 2016 Time Series, POST weight (V160102); n=2,641 voters, n=175 switchers. Ideology = 7-pt lib-con (V161126); “no position” = haven’t thought much about it. Switcher = crossed party lines in 2016 (V161158x × V162034a). Block area is proportional to share.
