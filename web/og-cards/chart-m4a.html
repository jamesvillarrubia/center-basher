The claim “Single-payer is a fringe idea with no real constituency.”
The “fringe” position is the most popular one in the country.
Where 2020 voters put themselves when the survey asks them to choose between a government insurance plan and a private one. More picked government insurance outright than picked any other point on the scale, including the private end.
Source: ANES 2020 Time Series, POST weight (V200010b), n=5,369 voters. The axis is the survey’s own 7-point government-vs-private medical insurance item (V201252); endpoint wording is verbatim. Candidate markers are the weighted mean position voters themselves assigned each nominee on the same scale (V201253 / V201254), not an editorial placement.
