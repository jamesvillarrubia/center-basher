Ideology decides who you vote for — not whether you show up.
Standardized weight of ideology in two 2016 models. It’s the dominant force in vote choice and essentially zero for turnout — so a “move to the center” can’t mobilize voters or expand the electorate.
Source: ANES 2016, weighted logistic models (n≈3,300); standardized effect |β·SD| of centered ideology.
