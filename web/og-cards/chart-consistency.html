The claim “Swing voters are moderates, so run as a moderate to win them.”
“Moderates” barely swing. 85% vote the same party every time.
Watch the inference run backwards. Even where swing voters look moderate, it does not follow that moderates swing. Of self-described moderates who voted in 2012, 2016 and 2020, 85% never once changed party.
Source: Democracy Fund VOTER Survey panel; self-described moderates who voted in 2012, 2016 & 2020; weighted.
