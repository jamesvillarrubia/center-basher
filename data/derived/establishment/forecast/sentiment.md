## 6. The leaders' own positioning

### Jeffries

Dear Colleague letter, 2026-11-04 (official copy: https://jeffries.house.gov/2026/11/04/dear-colleague-announcing-candidacy-for-speaker-of-the-house/). The fetch returned a summary, not verbatim text. Items below are the summary's, with quotes from outlets that printed them.

- Request: "I write to humbly request your support in connection with my candidacy" (Fox). He seeks to be the 57th Speaker.
- Opening line (Politico via Yahoo and Newsweek): "Against all odds, House Democrats were consistently successful in the minority. We must now deliver the dramatic change the American people are demanding in the majority."
- Record of the 119th Congress: ten actions, including opposing Medicaid cuts, a litigation task force, pressing for release of the Epstein files, a three-year extension of ACA tax credits, discharge petitions, opposing the Iran conflict, pushing for cabinet resignations, and a housing affordability bill.
- Three priorities: Affordability (five working groups: housing, healthcare, groceries and goods, gas and utilities, caregiving); Accountability (strengthening Congress as a check on the executive, price gouging, Supreme Court reform, a ban on member stock trading, holding people who exploit the public accountable); American Values (liberty, equal protection, voting rights, immigrant heritage, input from across the caucus).
- First 100 days: reverse Medicaid cuts, restore SNAP funding, extend ACA tax credits, end the Trump tariffs, stop the war in Iran.
- Bipartisanship: "try to find bipartisan common ground whenever and wherever possible" (Fox). On Republicans: "Donald Trump and House Republicans promised to lower costs on Day One. They failed."
- Seat expectation: Jeffries said he expects 255 or more Democrats (Fox); CBS has "at least 255" at 12:44 PM on 2026-11-04.
- Source conflict on the letter's oversight content: the official-page summary lists "accountability" items without naming investigations of Trump; a search summary of other coverage says the letter called for an aggressive oversight agenda including probes of Trump and his administration. The wording was not verified.
- The Axios copy returns 403: https://www.axios.com/2026/11/04/hakeem-jeffries-speaker-house-democrats-letter . Politico obtained the letter (per Newsweek).

Impeachment, oversight, "getting even":

- Press conference, Wednesday 2026-11-04: "I haven't had a single member of the House Democratic caucus talk to me about impeachment. We've been relentlessly focused on affordability and accountability." (search summary of New Republic coverage; https://newrepublic.com/post/217571/hakeem-jeffries-get-even-impeachment-democratic-agenda )
- MS NOW "Morning Joe," 2026-11-04: "The American people don't want us to get even. They want us to get things done on their behalf because life is way too expensive." Also: Democrats "ran on affordability, won on affordability, and now it's our job to govern on affordability." The "getting even" line came from the TV interview, not the press conference. https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/hakeem-jeffries-declares-getting-even-155425574.html
- Don Lemon interview: "We will deliver accountability"; anyone "screwing over the American people" would be held accountable on "day one of the new Congress." On ICE agents: "I never understood why they might be able to escape accountability." He noted the federal statute of limitations is five years. No impeachment answer is quoted. https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/deliver-accountability-preps-house-speaker-002130746.html
- August CNN interview: named the Judiciary and Oversight committees as leading on accountability and declined to rule impeachment in or out. Pre-election: "haven't ruled anything in, and we haven't ruled anything out." (search summary)

- Fox says Jeffries "left open" impeachment but said focus should be the economy (Yahoo "dramatic change" article).
- Source disagreement on framing: allies describe a focus on costs; critics describe avoidance of Trump oversight.
