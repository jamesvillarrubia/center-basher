## Year assignment and splitting rules

- Paragraph-style files (2003 onward): year is the section header ('2007:', 'Laws for calendar 2007').
- Flagged files (1981-2002): year is the last 4-digit year inside the entry text that falls in that Congress's two years. Entries with no such year have a blank `year` column; `congress` still comes from the list header.
- Title: text up to the first sentence-ending period (abbreviations such as G.I., St., No. skipped), or the first ' - ' in the 117th Congress file. If an entry has no period, the whole text is the title and the description is empty. Trailing years/months after the title stay in the description.
- Line-break hyphens are removed only when the joined word is in /usr/share/dict/words.
